851 GBai Marker: its terms allow commercial and non-commercial use, modification and redistribution, and turning it into a web font for your own site. We use only the Basic Latin range, with a narrower space and the punctuation re-spaced to fit its ink; no glyph outline was changed. The original font is from 8:51:22 pm.

斯科墨韵榜书: the font file itself is not served by this site. When the pages are built, each character a Chinese heading uses is drawn as a vector outline, the way a poster title is set; the heading text stays in the page, so screen readers still read it.

Film and TV data

By default, Nsuve's film and TV information comes from Nsurator Library DB, whose data comes from TMDB and Bangumi.

Film and TV data and images come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. themoviedb.org

Film artwork in the screenshots

Open-film artwork in the website screenshots is provided by Blender Studio, Blender Institute and Blender Foundation. Pepper & Carrot is created by David Revoy. Images are used under the individual licenses below (CC BY) and cropped, resized or converted to fit the interface.

Pepper & Carrot is an episodic webcomic, used here to illustrate the series interface; it is not a TV series. The screenshots contain demonstration content.
