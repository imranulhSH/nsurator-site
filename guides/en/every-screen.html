One library. Six screens.

The Mac is home base for your library; iPhone and iPad are the half you carry around. They can connect to your NAS and media servers on their own, or use Nsuve Link to play what's on your Mac.

What each screen does

Nsuve has the same name and the same purchase on every screen. The Mac version comes first. iPhone and iPad share one app, which iPhone Duo uses too, and Apple TV and Vision Pro versions are on the way.

On iPhone and iPad

The tab bar has Home, Sources, Files and Manage, plus a ＋ button. On iPad they sit in the sidebar on the left.

On a phone you can connect WebDAV, SMB, SFTP, Emby, Jellyfin and Plex, as well as files on the device and locations in the Files app. A Mac you've paired shows up under Sources too. Cloud drives and DLNA are Mac-only.

Sync between your iPhone and iPad with iCloud

In a source's settings, under Sync to Other Devices, turn on Sync This Source with iCloud. That source's recognition results, the matches you fixed by hand, the versions you picked, that NAS's settings and your watch progress then stay in step across the iPhones and iPads signed in to the same Apple Account. It all lives in your own iCloud, readable only by your devices. Passwords, Private Space and images are never synced.

Your Mac's library doesn't sync through iCloud. To watch your Mac's films on a phone, use Nsuve Link.

Nsuve Link: your Mac's films on your phone

Nsuve Link makes your Mac the center of your home library. A paired iPhone or iPad can browse the folders and posters the Mac shares and play them directly. By default it only accepts connections from your local network, and nothing goes through a cloud relay.

  1. On the Mac, click Nsuve Link in the workbench sidebar and turn on "Let other devices play videos from this Mac". It turns on by itself on your home network and pauses on other networks.
  2. Under Shared Folders, check the folders other devices may see. Click Add Device…, then Scan with iPhone. A QR code appears on the Mac.
  3. In Nsuve on your iPhone or iPad, go to Sources and choose Connect a Mac (the first time you open the app, Home also offers Connect Your Mac). Tap Scan and point at the QR code on the Mac's screen. If scanning doesn't work, try Look Nearby.
  4. The Mac shows that your phone wants to connect, along with a check code. If it matches the number on the phone, click Allow. Before you do, you can choose the folders it can see and whether to allow Save to Device, Convert on the Mac and Send to Inbox.

Once paired, the Mac appears under Sources on the phone; tap it to browse and play. A few things to know:

  • Only videos in your library folders are shared. Videos in Sources (external drives, local folders, NAS, cloud drives) won't appear on other devices, and Private Space and the Inbox are never shared.
  • When the Mac sleeps or Nsuve quits, other devices can't see anything. While it's playing to a device, the Mac won't go to sleep on its own.
  • When you watch your Mac's films on a phone, where you stop is remembered only on that phone.
  • To cut a device off, click Revoke… next to it in Nsuve Link on the Mac. Copies it already saved can't be taken back.

Send videos from your phone to the Mac

On iPhone, a file's ⋯ menu has Send to Mac. The video lands in the Mac's Inbox; once you file it into a shared folder on the Mac, the rest of the household can watch it too. Only videos can be sent, and subtitles don't go along for now. Keep Nsuve open while it sends; if the transfer breaks off, it picks up where it stopped. The Mac has to allow Send to Inbox for that device first.

Buy Pro once, use it everywhere

Pro belongs to your Apple Account, not to one device.

  • Pro yearly: free for the first 7 days, and it works on all your devices.
  • Lifetime Pro: for Mac, for iPhone + iPad, or for all platforms. All platforms covers Mac, iPhone and iPad, plus the Apple TV and Vision Pro versions when they arrive.
  • If you own one single-platform lifetime, you can upgrade to all platforms. If you own both the Mac and the iPhone + iPad lifetimes, you already have all platforms.
  • With only the iPhone + iPad lifetime, Nsuve on the Mac stays on Free.
  • Family Sharing isn't available at launch.

Free includes everything except AI tool connections, with 15 plays a month. A play counts once a video has actually played for 10 seconds; pausing and scrubbing don't count. The count resets on the 1st of each month, and each device keeps its own. Pro has no limit.
