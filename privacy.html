Nsurator Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Nsurator is designed as a local-first macOS video library manager.

Data Storage

Nsurator stores your video library metadata locally on your Mac. Your video files are not uploaded to Nsurator servers.

Personal Data

Nsurator does not sell personal data and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Files and Permissions

The app may request access to folders you choose so it can scan, organize, and manage local video files.

Network Access

Nsurator does not require an account to manage your local library. If future versions add online services, this policy will be updated before those services are used.
